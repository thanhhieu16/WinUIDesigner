## Results

Real app (walkthrough, c7b8ceb):
- **Plus twice** gave 150%. The thumbnail's frame shrank to match (`01f-zoomed-in.png`).
- **Fit** gave 82%: the 528px view over the 640px canvas, with the frame around the whole picture
  (`01g-fit.png`).
- **Ctrl+wheel**, two notches towards the user, took it from 82% to 68% (`01h-ctrl-wheel.png`).
- **100%** brought it back (`01i-back-to-100.png`). The rest of the walkthrough (placing,
  dragging, editing, the library steps) ran as before at 100%.

The bar's buttons sit at client y 711. Minus is at x 516, the percentage at 562, plus at 607,
100% at 656 and Fit at 706.

The first run stopped at the `ctrlwheel` step. That step sent the negative delta masked with
`0xFFFFFFFF`, which PowerShell reads as the int32 -1, so nothing was converted ("Cannot convert
-240 to UInt32"). It now goes through `BitConverter`.

The same CI run failed `ThumbnailViewTests` on a race. The 300ms timer's render was in flight
when the test called `RenderNowAsync`, which returned at once and let the test see the previous
picture. `RenderNowAsync` now awaits the render in flight, after asking it to go round once
more.
